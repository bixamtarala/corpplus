What CropPulse is becoming

CropPulse is designed to become the central digital layer for the agricultural value chain, connecting intelligence, decision-making, listings, buyer discovery, and workflow coordination in one platform.

  • Farmers: crop guidance, pricing visibility, selling timing, market access
  • Traders: supply discovery, demand visibility, faster coordination
  • FPOs: aggregation, coordination, and member market support
  • Customers: direct crop discovery and purchase workflows
  • Government-facing use: future alignment with digitization, transparency, and ecosystem modernization priorities
Active web surface Live Streamlit app with OTP flows, localization, marketplace and crop-selection experiences
Active mobile surface Native Flutter Android app with customer, farmer, trader, market, intelligence, and profile sections

Why investors should care

  • Large fragmented market: agriculture remains operationally fragmented across production, discovery, pricing, and execution
  • Credible wedge: CropPulse starts with intelligence and coordination, not generic marketplace claims
  • Visible execution: web, mobile, release automation, localization, and testing already exist in the repo
  • Platform logic: each new user group increases coordination value and data advantage
  • Capital efficiency: the team has shipped meaningful product breadth before building heavy infrastructure
Best fundraising message: CropPulse is not starting from idea stage. It is already a working agri intelligence product with early proof and a clear expansion path into the central coordination platform for agricultural participants.
