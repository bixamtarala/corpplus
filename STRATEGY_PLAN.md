## PROBLEM STATEMENT

### Current Agricultural Challenges
1. **Information Asymmetry**: Farmers/FPOs lack real-time market data for smart decisions
2. **Price Volatility Risk**: No AI-based early warning systems for commodity price changes
3. **Poor Timing Decisions**: Manual, delayed market information leads to suboptimal pricing
4. **Supply Chain Inefficiency**: Fragmented data, hard to track demand/inventory/procurement
5. **Financial Inclusion Gap**: Rural stakeholders can't access investment intelligence tools

### Pain Points for Core Users
- **Farmers & FPOs**: Don't know best timing to sell crops at peak prices
- **Agri-Traders**: Need demand forecasting to optimize inventory
- **Cooperatives**: Struggle with procurement timing & bulk pricing negotiations
- **Agri-MSMEs**: Limited access to market data for investment/scaling decisions

## CROPPULSE - FEATURE SET

### Module 1: Commodity Price Intelligence
**What It Does**:
- Real-time price tracking for key commodities (Rice, Cotton, Wheat, Sugar, Spices, Oils)
- Multi-market data aggregation (APMC, futures, wholesale, retail)
- Price trend analysis with 30/60/90 day forecasts
- Seasonal pattern recognition

**Dashboard Elements**:
- Price trend charts with AI projections
- Volatility indicators
- Market opportunity alerts (buy/sell signals)
- Geographic price comparisons

**User Benefit**: Farmers know WHEN to sell for maximum price

---

### Module 2: Demand & Supply Analytics
**What It Does**:
- Track crop demand patterns across regions/seasons
- Supply shortage/surplus prediction
- Market saturation alerts
- Procurement optimization recommendations

**Dashboard Elements**:
- Supply-demand balance charts
- Shortage/surplus forecasts
- Seasonal demand trends
- Regional opportunity maps

**User Benefit**: Traders can optimize inventory; FPOs can plan production

---

### Module 3: AI Risk Intelligence
**What It Does**:
- Weather risk forecasting (crop damage, price impact)
- Commodity price volatility alerts
- Procurement risk assessment
- Smart hedging recommendations

**Dashboard Elements**:
- Risk score dashboards
- Alert system (SMS/push notifications)
- Scenario analysis tools
- Impact forecasting

**User Benefit**: Early warning system prevents losses

---

### Module 4: Smart Recommendations Engine
**What It Does**:
- AI-powered timing suggestions ("Optimal window to sell: next 7-10 days")
- Pricing recommendations based on market conditions
- Procurement timing alerts
- Investment opportunity scoring

**Dashboard Elements**:
- Recommendation cards with reasoning
- Confidence scores
- Historical accuracy metrics
- "Why this recommendation" explanations

**User Benefit**: Removes guesswork from critical decisions
