## 🔍 KEY INSIGHTS ACROSS DOCUMENTS

### Strategic Insights
1. **Don't become another farm app** → Become the OS that connects all players
2. **Intelligence is the moat** → Data compounds over time
3. **Farmers are critical** → But traders generate initial revenue
4. **Network effects win** → Buy/sell matching makes it defensible
5. **Speed > perfection** → Get Phase 2 MVP in 4 months

### Technical Insights
1. **Streamlit → FastAPI** → React+Flutter (migration path)
2. **CSV → PostgreSQL** → Multi-database (evolution path)
3. **Single-user → Multi-user** → Microservices (scale path)
4. **OTP-based auth** → Standard for India market
5. **WhatsApp API** → Critical for 80% market accessibility

### Market Insights
1. **India's $150B agriculture market** is fragmented
2. **First integrated platform** wins huge first-mover advantage
3. **Farmer pain:** "When to sell" (biggest complaint)
4. **Trader need:** "Supply visibility" (competitive advantage)
5. **Expansion path:** India → South Asia → Global
