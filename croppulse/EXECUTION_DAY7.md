## 🎤 PRESENTATION PRACTICE (35 minutes)

### Setup (5 min)

**Practice Environment:**
1. Open pitch deck (full screen)
2. Have speaker notes visible
3. Imagine sitting across from 3 ICAR grant evaluators
4. Speak naturally (not robotic)

**What evaluators are thinking:**
- "Can this team execute?"
- "Is the problem real?"
- "Is the solution feasible?"
- "Will farmers actually use it?"
- "Is the business sustainable?"

---

### Practice Run 1: Smooth Reading (10 min)

**Steps:**
1. Read through entire pitch deck with speaker notes
2. Speak out loud (not silently)
3. Follow natural pace (not rushed)
4. Time yourself: Should be 15-20 minutes total
5. Focus on understanding, not memorization

**Pace Guide:**
- Slide 1-2: 90 seconds each (problem setup)
- Slide 3-5: 60 seconds each (solution intro)
- Slide 6-9: 75 seconds each (details)
- Slide 10-13: 60 seconds each (financials)
- Slide 14-15: 90 seconds each (ask + vision)

---

### Practice Run 2: Record Yourself (15 min)

**Steps:**
1. Record yourself presenting (phone camera, face visible)
2. Present full 15-20 minute pitch (full speed, no pauses)
3. Use only minimal notes (glance, don't read)
4. Watch back immediately after

**While watching, note:**
- [ ] Pace: Too fast? Too slow? Just right?
- [ ] Clarity: Easy to understand the problem?
- [ ] Confidence: Do you sound knowledgeable?
- [ ] Eye contact: Are you looking at "camera" (evaluators)?
- [ ] Energy: Do you sound passionate?
- [ ] Mistakes: Any major stumbles?

**What to improve for Run 3:**
- Slow down if you rushed
- Speak louder if mumbling
- More energy if monotone
- Clearer language if confusing

---

### Practice Run 3: Final Polish (10 min)

**Steps:**
1. Do one more full presentation (recorded)
2. This time, apply lessons from Run 2
3. Speak with confidence and clarity
4. Imagine the evaluator is your grandmother (explain simply, not technically)

**Quality checklist:**
- [ ] Entire presentation is 15-20 minutes
- [ ] Every slide feels natural (not rushed)
- [ ] Key numbers mentioned with emphasis
- [ ] Problem/Solution clearly explained
- [ ] Financial projections believable
- [ ] Ask (₹50L) is clear
- [ ] Closing is inspirational

## ❓ PREPARE FOR Q&A (10 minutes)

### Common Questions & Answers

**Q1: "What makes you different from other agritech companies?"**

**Answer:** "Three things: First, we focus on market intelligence, not on farming technology. Second, we're built specifically for Indian commodities and farmer workflows. Third, our freemium model makes it accessible - ₹0 for farmers, premium tier for those who want advanced features. Most solutions charge ₹50,000/month or more."

---

**Q2: "How will you acquire 10,000 users in Year 1?"**

**Answer:** "Through FPOs. We partner with farmer cooperatives who have existing membership networks. One FPO partnership reaches 500-2000 farmers instantly. We're targeting 10-15 FPO partnerships in Year 1. Beyond that, word-of-mouth from satisfied farmers, and eventually state agricultural department networks."

---

**Q3: "What's your unit economics? What does it cost to acquire one user?"**

**Answer:** "Our cost per user acquisition through FPOs is about ₹50-100 per farmer - mostly marketing and FPO partnership support. Our lifetime value is ₹500-2000 per user (assuming 20-30% premium adoption). So our CAC:LTV ratio is healthy - 1:5 to 1:20. We break even on individual users in 3-4 months."

---

**Q4: "Why Streamlit and not a 'proper' mobile app?"**

**Answer:** "Two reasons: Speed and reality. We needed to build and validate an MVP in 3 weeks. Streamlit let us do that. Mobile app would take 3+ months. Second, smartphone access in rural India is 82%, but it's often shared devices. Web-based is actually more practical. As we grow and validate demand, we'll build native mobile apps in Phase 2."

---

**Q5: "What happens if farmers don't adopt the app?"**

**Answer:** "We have contingencies: If direct farmer adoption is slow, we pivot to B2B - selling to FPOs, traders, and commodity brokers as a group licensing model. The underlying technology doesn't change, just the distribution channel. We've built relationships with all three user types, so we have multiple paths to market."

---

**Q6: "How do you ensure data accuracy? What if your price predictions are wrong?"**

**Answer:** "Good question. We're not doing price predictions - we're scoring risk and analyzing market conditions. Our algorithm is rule-based, not AI/ML, so it's fully auditable and explainable. We validate every rule against ICAR agricultural expertise. We also mark confidence levels so farmers know when to trust us most."

---

**Q7: "What's your go-to-market timeline? When will you be profitable?"**

**Answer:** "Month 1-3: MVP validation with 50-100 pilot farmers through FPO partners. Month 4-8: Scale to 5,000 users and launch premium tier. Month 9-12: Reach 10,000 users and achieve break-even. Year 2: Profitability with 50,000+ users. Year 3: Scale to 150,000+ users with ₹5+ Crore revenue."

---

**Q8: "Why should ICAR fund this vs. other opportunities?"**

**Answer:** "ICAR's mandate is to support market-linked innovation that reaches farmers. CropPulse does exactly that - it's market intelligence that helps farmers get better prices. We're not asking for ongoing subsidies; we're building a sustainable business. The grant funds acceleration and validation - not long-term dependence. Within 18 months, we're profitable and self-sufficient."
