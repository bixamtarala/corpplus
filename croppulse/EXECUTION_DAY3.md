## 🎙️ AUDIO SETUP (10 minutes)

### Test Your Microphone:
1. In Windows: Settings → Sound → Input devices
2. Click your microphone
3. Speak test words ("one, two, three")
4. Check recording level (green bar appears)
5. Adjust volume if too quiet/loud

### Audio Settings for Recording:
- **Input Device:** Your microphone
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1 kHz (or auto)
- **Bitrate:** 128 kbps (good quality)

### Recording Tips:
- **Pace:** Speak slowly (not rushed)
- **Clarity:** Speak clearly into microphone

- **Errors:** If you make a mistake, pause 2 seconds then continue (can edit later)
- **Confidence:** Imagine talking to farmers, not a camera

## ✂️ EDITING (45 minutes)

### Download Editing Software (FREE):
- **Windows:** DaVinci Resolve (https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)
- **Mac:** iMovie (built-in)

### Editing Steps:

1. **Import Video:**
   - Open DaVinci Resolve
   - File → Import → Select `croppulse_demo.mp4`

2. **Add Title Card (Opening):**
   - Timeline → Add title
   - Text: "CropPulse MVP Demo"
   - Duration: 2 seconds
   - Font size: 48, white text

3. **Add Text Overlays (during script):**
   - At 10 sec: "Real-Time KPIs"
   - At 20 sec: "30-Day Price Trend"
   - At 35 sec: "Intelligent Risk Assessment"
   - At 50 sec: "Real-Time Alerts"
   - At 70 sec: "AI-Powered Insights"
   - At 85 sec: "Multi-Commodity Support"
   - At 95 sec: "Data Export"

4. **Add Background Music:**
   - Download royalty-free music from:
     - YouTube Audio Library (youtube.com/audiolibrary)
     - Bensound.com
   - Volume: -15 to -12 dB (keep narration as primary)
   - Duration: Full 2 minutes

5. **Add Subtitles/Captions:**
   - Captions tab → Enable captions
   - Caption text: Repeat your narration
   - Duration: Match audio timing

6. **Add End Screen (Closing):**
   - Add another title
   - Text: "CropPulse - Agricultural Market Intelligence"
   - Subtitle: "croppulse.streamlit.app"
   - Duration: 3 seconds

7. **Export:**
   - File → Export
   - Settings:
     - Format: MP4
     - Resolution: 1920x1080
     - Frame Rate: 30fps
     - Codec: H.264
   - File name: `croppulse_demo_final.mp4`
   - Save to: `c:\Users\LENOVO\Desktop\Agritech\croppulse\`
