## Slide 6: Core Algorithm - Risk Scoring
**Title:** Intelligent Risk Assessment  
**Components (0-100 scale):**
- Volatility (40% weight): Price stability
- Price Change (30% weight): 7-day momentum
- Supply Gap (30% weight): Market balance

**Example:**
- Low Risk (0-30): Safe for selling
- Medium Risk (30-70): Monitor closely
- High Risk (70-100): Avoid selling

**Visual:** Risk meter graphic  
**Speaker Notes:** "Our proprietary algorithm analyzes three key factors to calculate risk. This gives farmers a single, easy-to-understand metric for decision-making."

## Presentation Tips
1. **Practice timing:** 15-20 minutes total (75-80 seconds per slide)
2. **Use speaker notes:** Practice with notes before presenting
3. **Emphasize numbers:** Farming population, market size, revenue projections
4. **Live demo:** Have Streamlit app open to show working product
5. **Q&A preparation:** Be ready to discuss tech stack, scalability, competition
6. **Storytelling:** Start with farmer story, problem, solution, traction
7. **Close with vision:** End on inspiring note about impact
