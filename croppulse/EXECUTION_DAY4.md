## 🎤 SPEAKER NOTES EXAMPLES

**Slide 1 (10 sec):**
"Thank you for having me. I'm excited to present CropPulse, an agricultural market intelligence platform that we've built to help India's 140 million farmers make better trading decisions."

**Slide 2 (60 sec):**
"Let me start with the problem. Indian farmers currently lack real-time market information. Most rely on local brokers or government data that updates daily at best. The result? Poor timing on sales. A farmer might harvest at the peak and sell at the valley, costing them ₹50,000 to 1,00,000 per season. For a smallholder making ₹2-3 Lakhs annually, that's devastating."

**Slide 7 (60 sec):**
"Our AI engine isn't magic - it's sophisticated rules-based logic. We analyze 10+ patterns: sustained uptrends over 7 and 14 days, price positioning relative to average, supply shortages below critical thresholds, and demand surges. Most importantly, each recommendation has a confidence score. When we say 'Perfect Storm' with 92% confidence - meaning shortage plus demand plus rising prices - that's 'sell immediately if possible.' When it's 60% confidence, farmers know to be cautious and monitor further."
