## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Setup Required
- **Screen Capture:** OBS Studio (free) or ScreenFlow (Mac)
- **Audio:** USB microphone (for clear narration)
- **Browser:** Chrome/Edge for best Streamlit performance
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 @ 30fps

### Recording Checklist
- [ ] Close all notifications and extra windows
- [ ] Streamlit app open and loaded
- [ ] Screen brightness at 80%
- [ ] Audio input level tested (-6dB to -3dB)
- [ ] Timer visible for pacing
- [ ] Mouse cursor visible and steady

### Editing
- Use DaVinci Resolve (free) or Adobe Premiere
- Add:
  - Opening title card (2 seconds)
  - Text overlays (timing with speech)
  - Background music (light, upbeat)
  - Logo watermark in corner
  - End screen with links
  - Subtitles/captions (for accessibility)
- Export as MP4 (H.264, 1920x1080, 30fps)
- Target file size: 30-50 MB

### Pacing Guide
- **Scene 1:** 10 seconds = Setup, calm opening
- **Scene 2:** 10 seconds = Show KPIs (let them breathe)
- **Scene 3:** 15 seconds = Charts require study time
- **Scene 4:** 15 seconds = Risk is complex, explain clearly
- **Scene 5:** 15 seconds = Alerts are important
- **Scene 6:** 20 seconds = Insights are the core value prop
- **Scene 7:** 10 seconds = Quick demo of flexibility
- **Scene 8:** 15 seconds = Export is bonus feature
- **Scene 9:** 10 seconds = Call to action, memorable close

### Audio Recording Tips
1. Record narration AFTER recording screen (easier to match timing)
2. Speak clearly at natural pace (not rushed)
3. Pause 0.5 seconds between sentences
4. Emphasize key numbers and features
5. Use warm, confident tone (not robotic)
6. Record in quiet room (close doors, minimize noise)
7. Do 2-3 practice takes before final recording

### Background Music
- Upbeat but not distracting
- Volume: -15 to -12 dB (narration is primary)
- Royalty-free sources:
  - Epidemic Sound (free trial)
  - YouTube Audio Library
  - Bensound.com
  - Incompetech.com
