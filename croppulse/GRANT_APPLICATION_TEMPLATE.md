## SECTION A: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Project Title
CropPulse - Agricultural Market Intelligence Platform for Commodity Stakeholders

### Project Duration
18 months (6 months R&D + 12 months market development)

### Funding Request
₹50 Lakhs (Five Million INR)

### One-Line Summary
A web-based platform providing real-time market intelligence, risk assessment, and AI-powered trading recommendations to empower 140 million Indian farmers to make better commodity trading decisions.

### Problem Statement
Indian farmers lack real-time market intelligence, causing poor timing on sales that costs ₹50,000-1,00,000+ per season. Current solutions are either expensive enterprise tools (₹50K+/month) or oversimplified. 82% of farmers use smartphones (NITI Aayog) but only 12% have access to market intelligence.

### Proposed Solution
CropPulse is a freemium platform that provides:
- Real-time commodity prices (Rice, Wheat, Cotton)
- Intelligent risk scoring (0-100 scale)
- AI-powered recommendations with confidence levels
- Supply/demand analysis
- Data export capabilities

### Expected Impact
- Reach 100,000+ farmers in Year 1
- Help farmers save ₹10+ Crore annually
- Create sustainable recurring revenue model
- Build foundation for India's AgriTech intelligence ecosystem

## SECTION D: TECHNOLOGY & METHODOLOGY

### Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Streamlit (Python-based, rapid development)
- **Backend:** Python (pandas, NumPy for data processing)
- **Data:** CSV-based (extendable to real-time APIs)
- **Hosting:** Streamlit Cloud (free, auto-scaling)
- **Deployment:** GitHub integration

**Rationale:** Rapid MVP development (3 weeks) with minimal infrastructure complexity.

### Algorithm Overview

#### Risk Scoring Algorithm (0-100 scale)
```
Risk Score = (Volatility × 0.4) + (Price Change × 0.3) + (Supply Gap × 0.3)

Where:
- Volatility (0-100): Historical price variability
- Price Change (0-100): 7-day momentum indicator
- Supply Gap (0-100): Market balance metric
```

**Result Interpretation:**
- 0-30: Safe (good selling window)
- 30-70: Monitor (neutral, hold decision)
- 70-100: Risky (avoid selling)

#### AI Insights Rules (10+ triggers)
1. Sustained uptrends (7-day + 14-day analysis)
2. Price positioning vs. average (±8% bands)
3. Supply shortage alerts (<35% critical)
4. Demand surge detection (>88% peak)
5. Volatility spike warnings
6. Composite patterns ("Perfect Storm")
7. Moving average crossovers
8. Momentum reversals
9. Historical comparison patterns
10. Confidence scoring for each insight (0-100%)

### Methodology
- **Phase 1 (Month 1-2):** MVP development + internal validation
- **Phase 2 (Month 3):** FPO pilot testing + feedback collection
- **Phase 3 (Month 4-8):** Regional expansion + premium features
- **Phase 4 (Month 9-12):** National scaling + partnership development
