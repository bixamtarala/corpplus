About CropPulse

CropPulse is building the central intelligence platform for Indian agriculture. We're creating the infrastructure that connects farmers, traders, logistics providers, and financial institutions into one seamless ecosystem.

🌾 Our Mission: Empower every participant in the agricultural value chain with real-time market intelligence, smart matching, and financial services.

Founded in 2026, CropPulse combines AI-powered price forecasting, supply-demand analysis, and network effects to create the first truly integrated agricultural marketplace.

Currently: Operating in major agricultural states with 500+ active traders. Phase 2 launches with 5,000 farmers and advanced marketplace matching.
