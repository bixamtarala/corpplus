Frequently Asked Questions

🔍 How accurate is the price data?

We provide 100% accurate real-time data sourced from AGMARKET, government databases, and commodity exchanges. Data is updated daily.

💰 Is CropPulse free to use?

Yes! CropPulse is completely free for farmers, traders, and FPOs. We believe agricultural intelligence should be accessible to everyone.

📱 Can I use it on my mobile phone?

Absolutely! Our dashboard is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

🔒 Is my data safe and private?

Yes. CropPulse doesn't store personal data. We only display market data. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

⚡ How often is data updated?

Market data is updated daily, usually at the end of trading hours. Historical trends are available for the past 30 days.

🎓 Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

No! CropPulse is designed for everyone. The interface is simple and intuitive. All charts and insights are explained in plain language.
